Salon Policy & Skin Consultation Guidelines

  • Medical Disclosure: Clients must inform their aesthetician of any existing health or skin conditions, including diabetes, epilepsy, hemophilia, heart conditions, active acne flare-ups, allergies, or recent dermatological peels.
  • Right to Refuse Service: Frenyz Salon reserves the right to refuse service if a client appears unwell, under the influence of alcohol/drugs, or if skin appears unfit for treatment.
  • Aftercare Responsibility: Detailed post-facial and post-wax aftercare guidelines will be explained. Frenyz Salon is not liable for complications arising from improper home aftercare.
  • GST Billing Structure: Frenyz Club member prices are completely inclusive of GST. For non-member guests, standard salon rates apply with GST calculated separately.
  • Membership Redemption: Member pricing benefits can only be redeemed within an active Frenyz Club membership.

Liposoluble wax is a premium strip wax enriched with vegetable oils and titanium dioxide, perfect for larger body areas such as arms, legs, and back with minimal pulling sensation. Brazilian wax is a stripless hot peel-off wax designed specially for delicate areas (bikini, underarms, facial zones); it clings exclusively to the hair shaft and not the skin, drastically reducing pain, redness, and irritation.
