Blog 131 - Balayage vs Highlights: Which Hair Colour Technique Suits Your Look?
Choosing between balayage and highlights depends on the hair colour effect you want, your natural hair shade, maintenance preferences, and personal style. Balayage creates a softer, blended, sun-kissed appearance, while traditional highlights offer more defined, evenly distributed lighter sections. Both techniques add dimension and brightness to your hair, but they differ in application, finish, and upkeep.
If you are considering a hair colour transformation, understanding these differences can help you choose a technique that complements your appearance and lifestyle.
What Is the Difference Between Balayage and Highlights?
Balayage and highlights are hair colouring techniques used to create lighter sections and dimension. The main difference is how the colour is applied and how the finished look appears.
Balayage: A freehand colouring technique in which a stylist paints colour onto selected hair sections to create a gradual, blended effect.
Highlights: A technique in which selected hair strands are lightened, commonly using foils to create more consistent and defined sections.
Balayage generally produces a softer transition between shades, whereas traditional highlights can create more noticeable contrast from the roots to the ends.
What Is Balayage Hair Colour?
Balayage is a French-inspired hair colouring technique that involves hand-painting colour onto selected strands. Instead of creating uniform sections from the roots, the stylist strategically places lighter shades throughout the hair.
This technique creates a naturally blended appearance, similar to the subtle lightening that hair can develop after sun exposure.
Key characteristics of balayage include:
Soft transitions between natural and lighter shades.
More brightness towards the mid-lengths and ends.
A dimensional finish without an overly uniform appearance.
Customisable colour placement based on haircut, hair texture, and natural shade.
A softer-looking regrowth line in many balayage styles.
Popular balayage shades include caramel, honey blonde, chocolate brown, ash blonde, and beige blonde. The right shade depends on your starting hair colour and desired result.
What Are Hair Highlights?
Hair highlights involve lightening selected strands to create contrast against the natural or base hair colour. Traditional foil highlights use foils to separate treated sections and help control the lightening process.
Unlike balayage, highlights can create a more uniform distribution of lighter strands throughout the hair, including closer to the roots.
Key characteristics of highlights include:
More defined and noticeable lighter sections.
Consistent colour placement throughout selected areas.
Greater control over brightness and contrast.
Options ranging from subtle, fine highlights to bold blonde sections.
The ability to create an overall brighter appearance.
Highlights are suitable for people who want a more noticeable colour change while retaining dimension in their natural hair.

What Are Hair Extensions?
Hair extensions are additional hair strands or sections attached to natural hair to create extra length, volume, or dimension. They are available in different materials, lengths, textures, and attachment methods.
Depending on your preferences, extensions can provide temporary styling changes or longer-lasting results.
Hair extensions are commonly used to:
Add length to short or medium-length hair.
Create fuller-looking hairstyles.
Add volume to thin or fine hair.
Experiment with different hairstyles.
Introduce colour effects without permanently colouring natural hair.
The final result depends on selecting extensions that blend naturally with your existing hair.
What Are the Different Types of Hair Extensions?
Different hair extension methods offer different levels of convenience, durability, and styling flexibility. Understanding their characteristics can help you identify which option suits your requirements.
1. Clip-In Hair Extensions
Clip-in extensions are temporary hairpieces attached using small clips. They can be applied and removed at home without permanent changes to natural hair.
Key advantages:
Easy to apply and remove.
Suitable for occasional styling.
Available in different lengths and colours.
No long-term attachment required.
They can provide a natural finish when the colour, texture, and placement match your existing hair.
2. Tape-In Hair Extensions
Tape-in extensions use adhesive strips to attach additional hair sections close to the roots. They are designed to lie relatively flat against the scalp, making them suitable for creating a seamless appearance.
Key advantages:
Flat attachment that can blend discreetly.
Suitable for adding length and volume.
Relatively quick salon application.
Natural movement when correctly installed.
Professional application and maintenance are important to prevent tangling and unnecessary tension.
3. Keratin Bond Hair Extensions
Keratin bond extensions use small sections of additional hair attached to natural hair with keratin-based bonds. The bonds are typically applied using specialised salon equipment.
Key advantages:
Individual strand placement.
Customisable positioning.
Suitable for detailed blending.
Flexible styling possibilities.
Because they involve individual attachment points, correct installation and removal are essential to minimise damage to natural hair.
4. Micro-Link Hair Extensions
Micro-link extensions, also called micro-bead extensions, are attached using small beads or rings that secure additional hair to natural strands.
Key advantages:
No adhesive required.
Individual sections can be positioned strategically.
Can offer natural movement.
Available in different hair lengths and textures.
The extensions require professional installation and regular maintenance to ensure the attachment points remain comfortable and secure.
5. Sew-In Hair Extensions
Sew-in extensions involve attaching additional hair to braided sections of natural hair using specialised stitching techniques.
Key advantages:
Suitable for adding significant volume.
Can provide a secure attachment.
Available in different textures and lengths.
Can support a variety of hairstyles.
The suitability of sew-in extensions depends on natural hair density, scalp comfort, and the tension created during installation.

Acrylic nails are artificial nail enhancements created by combining a liquid monomer with a polymer powder. This mixture forms a workable product that is shaped over the natural nail or an extension tip before hardening.
Acrylic nails are widely used for creating longer nail shapes and structured nail designs.
Key characteristics of acrylic nails include:
Firm and structured nail enhancement.
Suitable for creating longer extensions.
Customisable shapes and thickness.
Compatible with different nail colours and designs.
Requires professional shaping and finishing.
