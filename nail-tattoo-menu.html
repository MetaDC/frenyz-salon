Salon Policy & Tattoo Care Guidelines

  • Medical Disclosure: Clients must disclose any medical conditions that may affect Skin Treatments, Nail Treatments, or the Tattooing Process, including but not limited to diabetes, epilepsy, hemophilia, heart conditions, or sensitive skin allergies.
  • Sobriety & Fitness: Clients must not be under the influence of drugs or alcohol during the session. Frenyz Salon reserves the right to refuse service if the client appears in poor health or unfit for the procedure.
  • Aftercare Responsibility: Proper aftercare instructions and healing balms will be provided. Clients are responsible for following them to ensure clean healing and prevent complications. Frenyz Salon is not liable for issues due to improper aftercare.
  • Portfolio Photography: Frenyz Salon may take photographs of the completed nail art or tattoo for portfolio and promotional use, unless the client explicitly requests otherwise.
  • GST Billing Structure: GST will be exclusive for non-member prices only. All membership prices are completely inclusive of GST and can only be redeemed within active membership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about nail extensions, gel polish, and tattoo sessions at Frenyz Salon

At Frenyz Salon Vadodara, Semi Gel Extensions are ₹1,499, and full Acrylic Nails are ₹2,500. Standard high-gloss Gel Polish is ₹999, and luxurious Customized Bridal Nails for hands and feet are ₹4,999. Per-nail custom art starts from ₹50.

Semi Gel Extensions provide a natural, lightweight, and flexible feel that moves comfortably like your real nail, perfect for medium length and daily wear. Acrylic Nails combine a liquid monomer and polymer powder to form a hard, ultra-durable shell capable of dramatic lengths, complex shaping (stiletto/coffin), and heavy wear without breakage.

Our base starting rate is ₹1,000 per square inch. The final price is estimated during an in-person or WhatsApp consultation based on size, fine-line detailing, shading, color pigments, placement on the body, and custom artwork design.

Your safety is our absolute highest priority. We use 100% brand-new, sterile single-use needle cartridges opened directly in front of you, surgical-grade disinfectant protocols, premium hypoallergenic organic inks, and disposable barrier wraps for the entire tattoo station.

You can easily book online 24/7 via the official Zenoti Webstore, call our desk directly at +91 85600 24060 / +91 72650 01114, or start an instant chat on WhatsApp. Walk-in consultations are also welcomed at our Alkapuri salon.
