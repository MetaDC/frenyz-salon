Airbrush vs. High Definition (HD) Makeup

Both techniques ensure breathtaking, radiant skin, tailored with precision formulations to match your style and wedding moments.

Airbrush Makeup

  • Applied using a specialized micro-fine airbrush gun mist technique.
  • Lightweight, sheer, feather-soft, and natural radiant finish.
  • High water resistance, sweat-proof, and exceptionally long-lasting for long ceremonies.
  • Blends seamlessly into the skin, minimizing pores and micro-imperfections effortlessly.

High Definition (HD) Makeup

  • Specifically formulated for ultra-high-resolution cameras, 4K film, and flash photography.
  • Utilizes micronized, light-diffusing luxury pigments for a velvety, soft-focus finish.
  • Buildable coverage applied with professional artisanal brushes and beauty sponges.
  • Creates a flawless, pore-free complexion that looks natural up close and under intense stage lighting.
