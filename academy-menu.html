Freshers Foundation Course

COMPLETE HAIRDRESSING & SCIENCE DIPLOMA

15 WEEKS DIPLOMA
15 Weeks (5 Days a Week) Sat - Sun Live Salon Observation Theory • Demo • Practical Complete Tool Kit Included Certification + Portfolio Photoshoot

What Will You Learn? (Part 1)

  • Frenyz Philosophy: Core salon ethics, professional etiquette & client handling
  • Ethics of Hair Dressing: Professional conduct & hygiene protocols
  • Hair Science & Theory: Scalp anatomy, hair structure, growth cycles & trichology
  • Client Care & Consultation: Analyzing hair textures, expectations & face shapes
  • Lines, Layers, Graduation & Sectioning: Foundational geometric principles
  • Shampooing & Conditioning: In-depth scalp massage theory & practical application
  • Blow Dry & Thermal Styling:
    • Different types of blow dry & step-by-step procedures
    • Brush knowledge, heat control & sectioning
    • Ironing process & crimping techniques
    • Tong settings (different types of tonging curls) & roller settings

What Will You Learn? (Part 2)

  • Scissors Holding Techniques: Proper finger rest, biomechanics & clipper-over-comb
  • Universal Cutting Principle Lecture:
    • Women's & Men's cutting foundational techniques
    • Essential tools trichology & blade geometry
  • Color Wheel, Chart & Formulas: Color theory, undertones, neutralizing & reflections
  • Products Knowledge: Chemistry of salon formulations, shampoos, serums & masks
  • Knowledge of Texture & Treatments: Spa rituals, hair Botox & conditioning
  • Keratin Process & Theory: Smoothing systems, chemical restructuring & aftercare
  • Assessment Exams: Rigorous theory exams, mannequin tests & live model evaluations
Professional Tools Kit (INCLUDED WITH COURSE)

Professional Hairdressing Scissor, Hair Dryer, Straightening Iron, Set of Professional Combs & Brushes, Section Clips, Water Spray Bottle, and 1 Professional Dummy Head (for Hair Cutting practice).

ADDITIONAL: Salon Management Masterclass

Exclusive 1-Day specialized lecture conducted by Director Dheeraj Yadav on "How to Run a Successful Salon Business" – covering client retention, marketing, inventory, and team leadership.

Prerequisites: A commitment to Learning, Discipline, and Developing Through Practice.

Advance Creative Haircuts & Color Class

MASTER COLOR CHEMISTRY & ARCHITECTURAL CUTS

₹45,000/-
4 Weeks (5 Days a Week) Sat - Sun Live Salon Observation Theory • Demo • Practical Carry Own Salon Kit Certification + Portfolio Photoshoot

Master Color Chemistry

  • Color Theory Fundamentals: What are colors, primary & secondary colors
  • International Color Chart: Numbering systems, shade codes & level system
  • Undertones & Reflections: Warm vs cool tones, color neutralizing formulas
  • Developers (Hydrogen Peroxide): Volume strengths (10, 20, 30, 40 Vol) & lift ratios
  • Pre-Lighteners & Bleach: Safe de-colorizing without compromising hair integrity
  • Seven Stages of Lightening: Exact pigment transition stages & tonal deposits
  • Color Tubes & Types: Permanent, demi, semi & direct dyes
  • Color Application Techniques: Global, balayage, highlights, root touch-up & fashion tones

Advanced Architectural Cutting

  • Scissor Anatomy & Tools Trichology: Blade edges, tension screws & ergonomic balance
  • Lines, Layers, Graduation, Elevation & Angles: Geometric plane mapping
  • Secrets of Haircutting: Weight distribution, interior texture & perimeter control
  • Blunt Cut Precision: Sharp geometric square lines
  • Low & High Graduation: Controlled build-up of weight
  • Graduation Haircut (0° to 180°): Complete angle spectrum cuts
  • Haircut Shapes: Concave shapes, convex shapes & asymmetric structures
  • Assessment & Practical Exams: Comprehensive hands-on cutting and coloring tests
