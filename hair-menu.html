  • Clients must disclose any medical conditions that may affect Hair Treatments, Hair Coloring, or scalp health, including allergies, sensitive skin, or pre-existing conditions.
  • Clients must not be under the influence of drugs or alcohol during the session.
  • Frenyz Salon reserves the right to refuse service if the client appears to be in poor health or unfit for the procedure.
  • Proper aftercare instructions will be provided, and clients are responsible for following them to maintain long-lasting results.
  • Frenyz Salon may take photographs of completed styles for our portfolio and promotional showcase, unless explicitly requested otherwise.

Everything you need to know about our hair services, appointments, and membership in Vadodara.

At Frenyz Salon Vadodara, haircuts for Men range from ₹500 (Stylist) to ₹600 (Creative Director) for members, and ₹600 to ₹800 for non-members. Haircuts for Women range from ₹1,000 (Stylist) to ₹1,200 (Creative Director) for members, and ₹1,399 to ₹1,499 for non-members. Every haircut includes pre- and post-cleansing shampoo, deep conditioning, and signature styling.

Frenyz Salon is conveniently located on the 1st Floor, Vidhi Platinum, Jetalpur, Productivity Road, Alkapuri, Vadodara, Gujarat 390007. We are in the prime commercial hub of Alkapuri, readily accessible for clients from Jetalpur, Akota, Gotri, Old Padra Road, and greater Vadodara.

Frenyz Club members enjoy exclusive discounted pricing across all services, and all member prices are completely inclusive of GST. Non-member prices are standard salon walk-in rates where GST is applied separately. Membership also provides priority scheduling and special seasonal perks.
